Features

A desktop that feels made, not assembled.

Obryx takes the stability of Fedora and the power of KDE Plasma, then reshapes the parts you touch every day.

Home launcher

A Start-style Home that opens with the Windows key and gets out of the way once your app launches.

Smooth dock

A dock with continuous motion and softer animations, tuned so everything feels fluid.

Glass windows

Translucent glass styling with a graphite theme that stays readable, plus apps that run as separate windows.

Cyber Mode

A built-in mode for security tooling with native tools and optional, isolated Kali tool packs.

Widgets and settings

A native widget picker, a wallpaper picker and more settings to make the desktop yours.

Fedora underneath

Built on Fedora 45 with KDE Plasma, so you get current Linux drivers, apps and updates.

Install

Try it from a USB stick.

Obryx runs live from USB, so you can try it without changing anything on your computer.

Download the ISO

Use the download button above. It's one file, with nothing to unzip or combine.

Write it to a USB stick

On Windows, open Rufus, select the Obryx ISO and your USB stick, then press Start. On Mac or Linux, balenaEtcher works too. This erases the USB stick.

Boot from the USB

Restart your PC and open its boot menu (often F12, F11, F8 or Esc while it starts), then pick the USB stick.

Choose Try

Pick the live option to try Obryx. Nothing is installed to your drive until you choose to.
